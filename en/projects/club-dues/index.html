Club Dues – attendance and membership fees

Club Dues brings training attendance, membership fees, settlement status, groups and downloadable reports together in one application. Explore its actual interface or try the public demo.

Open public demo ↗Discuss Club Dues →

What problem does it solve?

Keeping attendance lists, group membership, fee amounts and payment records in separate spreadsheets creates repetitive administration and makes it harder to see what remains unpaid. Club Dues provides one workflow for these everyday tasks.

01

Attendance at training sessions

A coach selects a date, enters the session topic and records athletes’ attendance. The information can later be included in attendance reports.

02

Membership fees and settlements

The dues view shows amounts charged, payments settled and outstanding balances. Administrators can work with the fee amount for a selected month and review individual statuses.

03

Downloadable Excel reports

The reporting screen provides separate XLSX exports for fees and attendance. Users select a date range and download the relevant spreadsheet.

04

Group administration

An administrator can view groups and their monthly fees and add another group. The screenshots show the available interface, while the public demo lets visitors explore the application.
