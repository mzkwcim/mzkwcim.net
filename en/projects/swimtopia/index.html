Swimtopia – swimming training management

Plan swimming training, document attendance and completed tasks, then review the results in an individual athlete dashboard. Swimtopia was developed with support from KS Posnania Poznań and continues to evolve.

Ask about Swimtopia →Technical case study ↗

What problem does it solve?

The system brings training plans, session attendance, execution records and athlete statistics into one workflow. Its purpose is to support coaching decisions, not to replace the coach’s judgement or rank children against each other.

01

Training plans and microcycles

Coaches can prepare training sessions for their groups within a season plan. The history of previous sessions remains connected to the current plan, rather than scattered across disconnected spreadsheets.

02

Attendance and completed work

Recording that an athlete attended a session is only a starting point. Coaches can also document task execution, helping distinguish planned training from the work actually completed.

03

Individual training analysis

An athlete dashboard presents attendance, planned and completed volume, workload indicators, training categories, strokes and recent tests. These are pieces of context that a coach must interpret, not automatic judgements about performance.

04

Who is it for?

Swimtopia is intended for swimming coaches and clubs seeking a structured way to document and review training. A separate public demo is in preparation; the screenshots below show existing screens using anonymised example data.
